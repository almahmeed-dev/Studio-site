When to seek professional guidance

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a ketogenic diet, especially if you have diabetes or blood-sugar regulation concerns, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have kidney or liver disease, kidney stones, a history of disordered eating, take blood-pressure or heart medication, or have had your gallbladder removed.
