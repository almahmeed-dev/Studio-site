GlyphSweeper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

GlyphSweeper: Rogue Puzzle is developed by MubarakLab. No account is required, there are no ads, and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Information stored on your device

Your onboarding status, scores, best floor, moon shards, completed runs, Daily Rift streak, earned relics, equipped relic, sound setting, and haptic setting are stored locally on your device. MubarakLab does not receive this gameplay information. Deleting the app deletes this locally stored data unless it is restored from a device backup.

Diagnostics

Gameplay events are written only to Apple's local unified logging system to help diagnose reliability problems on the device. The app does not send these events to a MubarakLab analytics server and does not use a third-party advertising or behavioral analytics service. Apple may include diagnostic information if you separately choose to share analytics with Apple under your device settings.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes the optional Full Game Unlock purchase. GlyphSweeper uses RevenueCat to retrieve the available product and confirm or restore your lifetime entitlement. RevenueCat receives purchase and transaction information from Apple and an anonymous app-user identifier. We do not receive your payment-card information, name, email address, or gameplay data.

Data we do not collect

GlyphSweeper does not request or collect your contacts, photos, precise location, camera, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, or other sensitive personal information.

Tracking and advertising

GlyphSweeper contains no third-party advertising SDK and does not use data to track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.

Data retention and your choices

You control locally stored gameplay data by keeping or deleting the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records as needed to provide purchase restoration, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. For questions about an anonymous RevenueCat purchase record, contact us with your approximate purchase date and country or region.

Children

GlyphSweeper is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
