Hearthwild — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Hearthwild is developed by MubarakLab. A MubarakLab account is not required, the app contains no advertising, and MubarakLab does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Information stored on your device

Campaign progress, command and replay records, settings, restoration and recovery records, locally received Covenant Relays, blocked-player identifiers, and a randomly generated installation key used for aggregate in-app milestones are stored on your device. These records are not sent to a MubarakLab server. Deleting Hearthwild deletes them unless your device or operating-system backup restores them.

The local milestone ledger contains only timestamps and counts for events such as beginning or completing the free prologue, viewing the Full Living World offer, and purchase outcomes. It does not contain campaign content, board positions, your name, or an advertising identifier, and Hearthwild does not transmit it.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Full Living World purchase and provides Hearthwild with a verified entitlement so the purchase can be completed or restored. MubarakLab does not receive your payment-card information, Apple Account credentials, name, or email address. Apple's handling of App Store information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional Game Center and Covenant Relays

If you choose Game Center, Apple provides Hearthwild with your scoped Game Center player identifier and display name. To run a private turn-based Covenant Relay, Hearthwild sends the participating player identifiers, display names, match identifier, timestamps, and the replay-sealed Relay content through Apple's Game Center service to the other participant. Received Relay content and the other participant's identity may also be kept locally in Relay Harbor so you can reply, remove the Relay, or block that participant.

Game Center is optional. You can use the single-player campaign without it, remove individual Relays in the app, block or unblock Game Center participants, and manage Game Center through your Apple Account and device settings.

Private links and sharing

Hearthwild can create a private Covenant Relay link or share a response using the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination. The selected app or service handles the information you share under its own privacy terms.

Diagnostics and analytics

Hearthwild contains no third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. MubarakLab does not operate a gameplay analytics server for the app. Apple may provide diagnostic or performance information if you separately choose to share analytics with Apple in your device settings.

Data we do not collect

Hearthwild does not request your contacts, photos, precise location, camera, microphone, health data, payment-card information, or advertising identifier.

Children

Hearthwild is a general-audience strategy game. MubarakLab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Game Center availability and multiplayer restrictions are controlled by Apple Account and family settings.

Your choices

You can play without Game Center, decline sharing a Relay, remove local Relays, and delete the app to remove locally stored app data subject to device backups. App Store purchase history and Game Center data are managed by Apple.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.
