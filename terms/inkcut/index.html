Free download and InkCut Pro

Starting with version 1.2, InkCut is free to download. InkCut Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription). It removes the free plan's three SVG/PDF export limit and the small mark on PNG previews, and allows more than two color layers. The 7-Day Pro Trial is a free in-app purchase: it never charges, does not renew and cannot be restarted. When it ends, Pro features lock again unless you buy InkCut Pro, and your projects stay editable. If you bought InkCut while it was a paid download, you keep Pro at no cost. Apple handles prices, payment and refunds. Restore Purchases (Settings → InkCut Pro, or any InkCut Pro screen) brings Pro back on a device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Your artwork and outputs

Use artwork you own or are entitled to process. You control where you share your designs and editable backups. Retain backups of important work and check outputs before relying on them.

Physical designs

InkCut creates design files and does not operate cutting equipment. Geometric connectivity is not a material-strength or machine-safety certificate. Check physical scale in the destination software and print setup, and make a small test before fabrication.

Product boundaries

Tracing creates filled-region outlines, not centerline tool paths or editable fonts. Public SVG/PDF/PNG files are not editable-project backups. Check requirements in your receiving software before using an exported file.
