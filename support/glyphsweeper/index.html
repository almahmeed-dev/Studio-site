How to play

Numbers show how many shadow traps touch a tile. Explore tiles you know are safe, mark suspected traps, collect moon shards, and find the exit gate. Your first explored tile is always safe.

Restore the Full Game Unlock

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Open GlyphSweeper and tap the gear button.
  3. Choose Restore purchases.

The Full Game Unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase—there is no recurring subscription. If the App Store reports that the purchase succeeded but the game remains locked, restart the app and try Restore purchases again.

Refunds and billing

Apple handles all billing and refund decisions. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to review a purchase or request a refund.
