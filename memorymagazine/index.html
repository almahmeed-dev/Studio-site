Memory Magazine

Turn a weekend into a beautiful mini magazine.

For iPhone · Requires iOS 26 or later

Some weekends deserve more than a camera roll.

Start with your photos and a guided five-page story. Add the title, captions and small details you want to remember. Then shape the pages with layouts, colors, crops and cutouts.

You write the story. Memory Magazine gives it a place to live.

Make it yours

Choose still images from Photos or Files. Edit owned, optimized copies while your originals stay unchanged. Adjust layers, type and page details, or refine a photo mask with Erase and Restore. Optional subject-mask assistance runs on your device.

Read the cover on its own, then the following pages in pairs. Accessible reading presents your words and the image descriptions you supplied.

Keep a finished copy

Preview your actual output before saving or sharing. Choose PDF for A5, A4 or US Letter paper, or export PNG and JPEG pages at exact sizes. Multi-page image exports include an ordered ZIP and manifest, with optional text companions and contact sheets.

Export checks flag missing images, text that does not fit and photos that may look soft. Your screen size and zoom do not change the page artwork.

Your magazine, on your device

No app account, ads or external AI processing. Editing and rendering happen locally after import. There is no built-in cloud sync; Photos and Files providers, purchases and destinations you choose may use a connection.

Keep a private editable backup for moving or restoring your work. Backups include full optimized source images and private notes, so they deserve more care than a finished PDF. A separate recovery-copy tool can preserve raw library files and unfinished work, but it is not a restorable editable backup. Read what each kind of file contains.

Start free. Grow with Pro.

Free includes two active magazines, up to eight pages each, Weekend Editorial, core editing tools, every export format and editable backups, without a watermark. Archive a magazine to free an active slot.

Memory Magazine Pro is a single purchase for up to 50 active magazines, up to 40 pages each, all three original styles and saving new custom style presets. The intended U.S. price is US$29.99; the app shows the actual localized App Store price before purchase.

Existing work remains available if purchase access changes. Current access is required for new restricted actions.
