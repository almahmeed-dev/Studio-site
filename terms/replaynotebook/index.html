Personal practice review

Replay Notebook helps you plan, record or import, manually compare, annotate and reflect on practice. You retain ownership of your content. Record, import and share only material you have the right to use and obtain any required consent from people you record.

The app records your observations. It does not identify or score technique, guarantee sports improvement, diagnose conditions or provide medical advice. Custom sport labels are organizational. Drawings are fixed image marks, and 2D angles depend on camera perspective rather than calibrated joint measurements.

The app requires iOS 26 or later. Recording depends on supported camera formats, permissions, storage and device conditions. Public MP4 exports are 1080p SDR at 30 fps, up to 120 final seconds. Narration is limited to 60 final seconds over its locked review. Voice memos are separate and limited to 120 seconds. Inspect the output before sharing it.

Free and Pro

Free includes 3 active sessions and 30 active attempts with the core manual comparison, annotation, full-quality export, memo, narration and editable-backup tools. Archive can make room within active limits without removing media. Archived work remains viewable, editable, exportable and usable as a reference.

Optional Replay Notebook Pro adds active capacity and saving custom capture templates, review filters and export styles. Monthly and annual plans unlock the same Pro features. Pro is not unlimited device storage; the app's operational library limit is 2,000 stored attempts, including active, archived and Trash records.

When Pro ends or is revoked, existing work is not hidden, deleted or automatically archived. Previously saved configurations remain usable and editable. New Pro operations may be limited. An accepted export can finish after expiry.

Subscriptions

Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Apple shows the available localized total and billing period before confirmation. An annual plan is billed for the full year. No introductory trial is offered.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple account. Apple processes billing under its Media Services terms. Open Settings → Pro and purchases in Replay Notebook to manage your subscription, or use Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app or notebook does not cancel billing.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used to subscribe. Pending or unverified purchases do not grant access until a valid entitlement is confirmed. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com under its policies and your applicable rights.

Your content and backups

Keep a separate backup of work you want to preserve. A .replaynotebook archive includes editable journal data and required media, potentially including private notes from additional referenced sessions. The app does not encrypt or password-protect these archives. Review the scope and recipient before sharing.

There is no app account or developer server backup. Permanent deletion or uninstalling the app can remove your local work. The app cannot delete external copies you previously saved or sent. See the privacy policy for storage, permissions and support correspondence.
