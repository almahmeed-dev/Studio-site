From a few words to a finished video

  1. Plan. Write or import a script and organize it into shot cards.
  2. Record. Follow the on-screen prompter or import your own footage; HDR clips are converted to SDR on your iPhone.
  3. Choose and edit. Compare takes, trim, reorder and frame your clips while preserving originals.
  4. Caption and export. Edit captions manually and save portrait, square or landscape video.

Exports are 1080p SDR MP4 at 30 fps, up to five minutes. Optional automatic transcription runs on the device when supported hardware, language and Apple model assets are available. The complete manual workflow works without speech models.

Keep your work yours

No Take Studio account, advertising or app analytics. Projects live on your iPhone; you choose where to save exports and editable project backups. Device backups and services you choose may keep copies under their own settings.

The free plan includes one active project and the complete manual editing, captioning and export workflow. Archive a project to make room for another. Existing and archived projects stay editable and exportable.

Take Studio Pro adds more active projects and reusable caption brand presets, removes the end card and lifts the free-use limit on Tighten for speaking. Where available, monthly and annual plans unlock the same features. Available prices and any eligible trial are shown by Apple before purchase.

FAQ

What is Take Studio?

A teleprompter, a take library and a caption editor inside one iPhone project. Write or import a script, record with the prompter, keep the takes that land, edit them and export a captioned video without switching apps.

Does the teleprompter text show up in my video?

No. The prompt and the record controls never appear in your footage. You set the scroll speed (half to double), text size, line width and reading guide, and you can drag the prompt up near the lens.

Can I use it with a beam-splitter teleprompter rig?

Yes. You can mirror the script text for a beam-splitter rig, or keep selfie recordings mirrored.

Can I edit clips I shot earlier?

Yes. Import clips from Photos or Files. HDR clips are converted to SDR on your iPhone when you import them. Your original recordings are never altered.

How do captions work?

Type captions yourself, or transcribe on the device where your device and language support it (a one-time system speech download may be needed). Fix any word, adjust timing and style, then burn the captions into the export or leave them out. Captions are your own spoken words; Take Studio does not translate.

What can I export?

Vertical 9:16, square 1:1 or widescreen 16:9 video, up to five minutes, as a 1080p SDR MP4 at 30 fps. Save it to Photos or Files or send it through the share sheet.

What is free?

Recording, take selection, editing, captioning and export, with one active project at a time. Archive a finished project to start another; archived projects can still be edited and exported. Tighten for speaking has three free uses. If your first install is version 1.3 or later and you use the free plan, each export ends with a 2-second “Made with Take Studio” card. Nothing is drawn over your recording, and people who used Take Studio before 1.3 never get the card.

What does Take Studio Pro add?

Several active projects, reusable caption brand presets, no end card on new exports and unlimited Tighten for speaking. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, and the yearly plan includes a free 1-week trial for eligible new subscribers. If Pro lapses, every project, take, export and backup stays yours to open and export.

Does Tighten for speaking use AI?

No. It uses Take Studio's own rules to trim filler, wordy phrases and long sentences. Every change is shown before it replaces your words, and you can undo it.

Do I need an account? Are my videos uploaded?

No account, and nothing is uploaded by the app. Projects live on your iPhone; you choose where exports and project backups go. Backup archives are not encrypted.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. On iPhone Duo, the preview and editing tools share the wider screen.

Where Take Studio fits

Take Studio covers short spoken videos from script to export. Here is how it compares with the tools people often combine instead.

Standalone teleprompter apps
They prompt and record. Take Studio adds take comparison, the edit and captions in the same project, so you don't move files between apps.
Full video editors
They offer multiple tracks, effects and longer timelines. Take Studio keeps to one spoken-video edit of up to five minutes at 1080p, which keeps it quick.
Editors built into social apps
They post straight to that platform. Take Studio exports a standard MP4 you can post anywhere or keep.
Online captioning services
They usually need an upload and an account. Take Studio transcribes on the device where supported, and you can always type captions by hand.
