Free download and Kerfwise Pro

Kerfwise 1.0 and 1.1 were sold as a paid download with every feature included. Starting with version 1.2, Kerfwise is free to download. The free plan keeps one active project at a time with full planning, verified plans, layouts, the cut order and plan PDFs; each page of a free-plan PDF ends with a small “Cut plan made with Kerfwise” line and an App Store link.

Kerfwise Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It adds unlimited active projects, reserving stock and recording actual cuts and kept offcuts, plan CSV files, QR label sheets and plan PDFs without that line. The 7-Day Pro Trial is a free in-app purchase: it never charges, does not renew and cannot be started twice. When it ends, the Pro tools lock again unless you buy Kerfwise Pro; projects, plans and stock records you made stay open and editable.

Paid for CutBench (now Kerfwise) before version 1.2? That purchase already includes Pro. The app confirms this on your device with the App Store. Restore Purchases in Settings → Kerfwise Pro brings Pro back on another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

Apple processes purchases, redownloads and refund requests and shows the current price and purchase terms before you buy. There is no Kerfwise account. There is no promise of perpetual operating-system compatibility or unlimited future major versions. Redownloading the app and restoring saved workshop data are separate operations.

Planning scope

Kerfwise produces geometric plans from the material, thickness, dimensions and constraints you enter. It supports rectangular guillotine sheet partitions and straight cuts with fixed board cross sections. Arbitrary polygon nesting and machine control are outside its scope.

Complete candidates are independently verified for the entered geometry. A feasible candidate does not prove minimum waste or optimality, and a bounded search that finds no candidate does not prove impossibility. Drawings are schematic rather than full-size cutting templates.

Check measured material, grain, permitted rotation, kerf and inclusive edge trims before using a plan. Kerfwise does not certify machine setup, furniture strength or safe workholding. Follow your equipment’s instructions and use your own judgment in physical work.

Actual cuts and retained records

Confirm a cut only after that physical cut has happened. Confirmation records consumption of a unique parent piece and cannot be undone into uncut stock. Corrections append to the history. If a save fails, retry the same operation and measurements without cutting again.

Only measured offcuts explicitly kept become available material. Restored and CSV-imported stock requires physical identity reconciliation. Keep a verified private backup and resolve pending physical work in the original workshop before relying on a backup.
