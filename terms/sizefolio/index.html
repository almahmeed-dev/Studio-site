Using Sizefolio

Sizefolio helps you import supported images and selected PDF pages, review physical dimensions, arrange sheets or poster tiles and generate PDFs on iPhone. You keep ownership of content you create. Import, print and share only material you have the right to use.

The app requires iOS 26 or later. Source, resource and job limits apply, and unsupported PDF content is rejected rather than silently removed. Import, generation and saving depend on available storage, provider access and device conditions. Review the actual saved output before relying on it.

Dimensions and printer results

Intended frame dimensions, the image drawn inside a frame and a receiving printer’s measured output are separate. Item Fit/Fill/Stretch choices differ from the explicit whole-output Fit option. Review rounding, paper, margins, content and every generated page. To preserve the reviewed PDF dimensions in another print app, choose Actual size or 100% and check the printer’s paper and scaling settings.

Direct AirPrint and printer correction inside Sizefolio are currently unavailable. Save or share a PDF to print through another app. Document reference marks and measurement entry do not activate correction. A PDF preview or system callback does not establish a measured paper result.

Your content and editable backups

Keep a separate editable backup of work you want to preserve. A backup can contain complete owned source PDFs, unselected pages, cropped-out image/PDF content, private notes, versions, templates and output records. Backups are not encrypted or password-protected. Crops are visual clipping, not secure redaction; share only content you intend the recipient to receive.

A finished PDF is separate from editable app data. Uninstalling the app or permanently deleting work can remove local data. Copies saved outside the app must be managed separately. There is no developer server backup of your library. See the privacy policy for storage, deletion, sharing and support-message details.
