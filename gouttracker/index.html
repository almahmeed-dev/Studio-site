GoutTracker

Record clearly. See what is missing. Prepare for care.

GoutTracker helps you record chosen food portions and times, water volumes, uric-acid results, and symptom episodes. Your basic editable history remains available without a subscription and without an account.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Shows the calculation and source status behind each recorded food estimate.
  • Separates unknown, partial, and explicitly completed diary days.
  • Preserves original uric-acid values and units while charting against a target you record.
  • Keeps one editable symptom episode with onset uncertainty and resolution.
  • Creates a previewable appointment report with coverage and uncertainty stated plainly.

Free and Pro

Food lookup and editable food, water, lab, and symptom history are free. Pro adds weekly coverage summaries, uric-acid trend charts, descriptive pre-symptom history, and flexible appointment reports. Any trial is shown only when Apple and RevenueCat confirm that the account is eligible; the Apple purchase sheet shows the final terms.

Sources and limits

Food rows are labeled source-verified only when they include an exact reviewed citation; other rows remain estimates. Recorded purine is not an overall gout-risk score, and a food recorded before symptoms is not proof of a trigger or cause. GoutTracker is educational and does not diagnose gout, prescribe treatment, or replace a clinician.
