From a few words to a finished video

  1. Plan. Write or import a script and organize it into shot cards.
  2. Record. Follow the on-screen prompter or import your own footage; HDR clips are converted to SDR on your iPhone.
  3. Choose and edit. Compare takes, trim, reorder and frame your clips while preserving originals.
  4. Caption and export. Edit captions manually and save portrait, square or landscape video.

Exports are 1080p SDR MP4 at 30 fps, up to five minutes. Optional automatic transcription runs on the device when supported hardware, language and Apple model assets are available. The complete manual workflow works without speech models.

Keep your work yours

No Take Studio account, advertising or app analytics. Projects live on your iPhone; you choose where to save exports and editable project backups. Device backups and services you choose may keep copies under their own settings.

The free plan includes one active project and the complete manual editing, captioning and export workflow. Archive a project to make room for another. Existing and archived projects stay editable and exportable.

Take Studio Pro adds more active projects and reusable caption brand presets, removes the end card and lifts the free-use limit on Tighten for speaking. Where available, monthly and annual plans unlock the same features. Available prices and any eligible trial are shown by Apple before purchase.
