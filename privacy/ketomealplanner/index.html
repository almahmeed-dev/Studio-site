Keto Meal Planner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Keto Meal Planner is developed by MubarakLab. It requires no MubarakLab account, contains no advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Dietary preferences, exclusions, favorites, accepted meal plans, shopping checklists, pantry choices, meal logs, weight entries, and app settings are stored locally on your device. MubarakLab does not automatically receive this information. Recipe data is bundled with the app for offline use.

Optional product analytics

When Share Anonymous Usage is enabled in Settings, TelemetryDeck receives limited product-interaction and device-context data for analytics, such as app launch, onboarding progress, feature use, shopping progress, and purchase-flow outcomes. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. Events do not include recipe names, allergy selections, body weight, health values, or meal intake. You can turn this off in Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat receives purchase-history information needed for app functionality and analytics, including checking entitlement access, restoring purchases, measuring purchase outcomes, and supporting attribution. This data is not used by Keto Meal Planner for tracking and is not linked to an app account because the app has no account system. MubarakLab does not receive payment-card information or Apple Account credentials.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional and requires your permission. The current app can write body weight and dietary energy when in-app sync is enabled; it does not request permission to read other Apple Health records. Health values are not sent to TelemetryDeck or RevenueCat. You can stop new writes in the app and change permission in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Exports, support, and deletion

You can deliberately export a local backup or recipe PDF, then choose where iOS saves or sends it. The receiving app or service handles that copy under its own terms. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the information you choose to send. You can edit or delete local plans and logs; uninstalling the app removes its local container, subject to iOS backup behavior.

Retention, security, and sharing

Local data remains until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to device backups. Apple, RevenueCat, TelemetryDeck, and email providers retain transmitted data under their own policies. MubarakLab does not sell personal information. Limited data is shared only with those providers to operate the features described above or when legally required.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13, and MubarakLab does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Material policy changes will be posted here with a revised date.
