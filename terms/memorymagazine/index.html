Your photos and words

Your content remains yours. Choose content that you own or have permission to use, and consider the privacy of people whose images or personal information you include. You decide what to put on a page and what to save or share.

Memory Magazine is a local editing and export tool. It does not provide an app account, developer-hosted storage, automatic publishing or a printing service. Imported photos are optimized copies; the app leaves originals unchanged. Crops and masks are visual editing tools, not secure redaction.

Free and Pro

Free includes two active magazines, up to eight pages per magazine, Weekend Editorial, core editing tools, all public export formats and editable backups, without a watermark. Archive keeps work locally while freeing an active slot.

Memory Magazine Pro is one nonconsumable purchase. It raises limits to 50 active magazines and 40 pages per magazine, adds all three original styles, and permits saving new custom style presets. The intended U.S. price is US$29.99. The actual localized App Store price is displayed before purchase.

Apple handles payment and purchase verification. A pending, canceled or unverified purchase does not unlock Pro. Restore purchases checks access for the current App Store account; it does not restore magazine files. Backups do not grant purchase access.

If purchase access changes, existing work remains available. New actions that require Pro remain subject to current access and the applicable limits. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem service; Apple handles refund decisions.

Saving, backups and recovery

Keep an accessible editable backup before removing the app or changing devices. There is no built-in cloud sync. A local library may be lost if the app is removed or the device is lost. Device-backup services and cloud file providers have their own settings and terms.

Public exports contain selected visible page content. Private editable backups contain broader material, including private notes and full optimized source photos outside visible crops and masks. Raw recovery copies may additionally contain unfinished drafts, existing outputs and damaged database or recovery bytes.

Editable and raw recovery archives are not encrypted by Memory Magazine. A raw .memoryrecovery archive is not a validated editable backup: checking its bytes does not establish that its contents can be restored, does not repair the library and does not make it compatible with editable backup restore.

Saving or sharing is your choice. Check the contents and chosen destination before sending a file. Creating a local archive does not automatically send it to support. Deleting current work does not delete private archives or copies previously saved or shared elsewhere. See the privacy policy for the contents and retention distinctions.
