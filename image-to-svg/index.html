Questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The page reads your picture with your browser, traces it with JavaScript on your device and saves the SVG straight from memory. It has no upload code, and its security policy blocks it from opening network connections. Once the page has loaded, the converter also works offline.

Is it free, and what are the limits?

It is free, with no account, watermark or daily limit. To keep it fast, pictures are scaled down to 1600 pixels on the long side before tracing, and the output has one or two colors.

Will the SVG work with my cutting machine or laser software?

The file is plain SVG made of filled paths, which design and cutting software that imports SVG can open. It is measured in pixels, not millimeters or inches, so set the size after importing and cut a small test piece first.

Which pictures trace best?

High-contrast artwork: ink drawings, logos, silhouettes and clear scans. Photos usually need the threshold moved and specks removed. Your browser decides which file types it can open; JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF work in current browsers.

Can it make single-line paths or more than two colors?

No. It traces the outlines of filled shapes in one or two colors. It does not make single-line centerline paths. For up to six colors on separate layers and real-world sizes, the Stencilsmith iPhone app goes further.
