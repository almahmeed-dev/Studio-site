Keto Meal Planner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Keto Meal Planner is developed by MubarakLab. It requires no MubarakLab account, contains no advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Dietary preferences, exclusions, favorites, accepted meal plans, shopping checklists, pantry choices, meal logs, weight entries, and app settings are stored locally on your device. MubarakLab does not automatically receive this information. Recipe data is bundled with the app for offline use. On version 4.0 and later the app also keeps, on your device only: a record of when it was first installed and whether data from an earlier version was present (in app storage and the iOS Keychain), the answer to an App Store check of your original download (made on your device through Apple's StoreKit), your pantry selections, a copy of today's meals and your shopping list in the app's shared container for widgets and Siri, and Spotlight entries for your own recipes. The first two decide which free plan applies to you; none of this is sent to MubarakLab or anyone else.

Analytics (version 3.7 and earlier)

Version 4.0 and later contain no analytics: nothing about how you use the app is sent to MubarakLab or anyone else, and the first launch removes the old usage-statistics settings from your device. In version 3.7 and earlier, when Share Anonymous Usage was enabled in Settings, TelemetryDeck received limited product-interaction and device-context data for analytics, such as app launch, onboarding progress, feature use, shopping progress, and purchase-flow outcomes. This data was not linked to your identity and was not used for tracking, and events did not include recipe names, allergy selections, body weight, health values, or meal intake.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. Version 4.0 and later check your subscription only with Apple's StoreKit on your device. In version 3.7 and earlier, RevenueCat also received purchase-history information for checking entitlement access, restoring purchases, measuring purchase outcomes and attribution; it was not linked to an app account because the app has no account system. MubarakLab does not receive payment-card information or Apple Account credentials.

On-device suggestions

On devices where Apple Intelligence is available and turned on, version 4.0 and later may ask Apple's on-device model to choose among the app's own matches in Cook from your pantry. The request holds ingredient and recipe names only and is processed on your device.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional and requires your permission. The current app can write body weight and dietary energy when in-app sync is enabled; it does not request permission to read other Apple Health records. The app never sends Health values anywhere. You can stop new writes in the app and change permission in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Exports, support, and deletion

You can deliberately export a local backup or recipe PDF, then choose where iOS saves or sends it. The receiving app or service handles that copy under its own terms. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the information you choose to send. You can edit or delete local plans and logs; uninstalling the app removes its local container, subject to iOS backup behavior.

Retention, security, and sharing

Local data remains until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to device backups. Apple, email providers and, for version 3.7 and earlier, RevenueCat and TelemetryDeck retain transmitted data under their own policies. MubarakLab does not sell personal information. Limited data is shared only with those providers to operate the features described above or when legally required.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13, and MubarakLab does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Material policy changes will be posted here with a revised date.
