InkCut — Privacy policy

Local artwork processing; choices when you share.

InkCut · Support · Privacy · Terms

Publisher: Mubarak Almahmeed. Effective date: October 5, 2026. Privacy contact: almahmeed.dev@gmail.com.

Artwork stored and processed on your device

InkCut processes source images, cleanup, tracing, editable vectors and file generation on your device. It stores project content, normalized source assets, revisions and recovery records locally. InkCut has no account, advertising or analytics. Your artwork is processed locally without an external AI service.

Permissions and selected files

Camera access is requested when you choose capture. Photos uses the system selected-item picker; Files uses your explicit selections. Providers may use their own accounts and networks. The image kept by InkCut excludes the selected file’s provider name, EXIF/GPS information and DPI. Export files can include design dimensions and labels you choose.

Exports and editable backups

You choose whether and where to save or share. Public SVG/PDF/PNG files contain the exported design and chosen labels, excluding the original source image, private notes and recovery history. Exported designs can themselves reveal personal content you drew. Editable .inkcut backups intentionally contain project/source content, private notes and saved versions, plus project identifiers, format information and integrity checksums used for validated restore. Share them only with recipients you choose.

System services and local security

Device backups, selected file providers and receiving apps can keep copies according to their settings. InkCut does not offer its own cloud synchronization.

Managing data

Archive and Trash organize local projects; permanent deletion is a separate reviewed action. Removing local data does not remove previously exported/shared/provider copies. Deleting the app may remove local work. Keep an editable backup separately.

Support communications

If you email support, Mubarak Almahmeed receives the information you choose to send through email services. Include only what is needed to answer your question; avoid sending private artwork or complete backups unless necessary. You can request deletion of support messages by emailing almahmeed.dev@gmail.com. InkCut never sends support messages automatically.

Changes and questions

Questions about this policy or your support communications: almahmeed.dev@gmail.com. This page will be updated when our practices change.
