RoomFit Pro

RoomFit Pro is an optional one-time nonconsumable App Store purchase. The purchase screen shows Apple’s current localized price and requires your confirmation. Use Restore purchases in the app if you already bought it with your Apple Account.

Pro adds capacity for up to 50 active projects and 10 alternatives per project, plus saving custom furniture and palette presets. Existing work remains accessible when an entitlement changes.

Planning with RoomFit

RoomFit provides 2D planning findings using measurements, dimensions and prices you enter. Verify actual dimensions, access routes, assembly space and final charges before purchasing furniture. Photos and style boards are visual references.

For information about local data and your sharing choices, read the privacy policy. For help with the app, visit RoomFit support or email almahmeed.dev@gmail.com.
