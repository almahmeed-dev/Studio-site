From source to printable PDF

  1. Import. Select a supported still image from Photos or Files, or choose supported PDF pages. Review any required image reduction.
  2. Set the frame. Enter millimetres or inches, including supported fractional inches, and confirm the intended physical dimensions. Choose Fit, Fill or Stretch inside the frame; crop visually, rotate or mirror the item.
  3. Arrange. Mix sizes on a sheet, create a repeat grid with reviewed gaps, or inspect poster overlaps and partial edge tiles.
  4. Inspect and save. Review every generated PDF page, then save to Files or choose a sharing recipient. Keep templates, named versions and editable backups separately.

An item frame and the image drawn inside it are different dimensions. The app also offers an explicit whole-output Fit option with scale review; it is off by default. Ordinary exported PDFs are canonical and uncompensated. Choose Actual size or 100% in the receiving print app to preserve the PDF’s reviewed dimensions; a printer’s measured result depends on its paper and settings.

Printing from this version: save or share the generated PDF and open it in your chosen print app. Direct AirPrint and printer correction inside Sizefolio are currently unavailable. Document reference marks and measurement entry do not activate printer correction.

Keep useful layouts

Jobs, owned sources, versions and retained outputs are stored locally. You choose where to save PDFs and editable backups. Providers, recipients and system device backups can keep copies under their own settings.

Cropping controls visibility and is not secure redaction. An editable backup can contain complete source PDFs, unselected pages, cropped-out content and private notes. Keep a trusted editable backup when you want to preserve the job; a finished PDF is a separate document.
